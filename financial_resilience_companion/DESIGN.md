## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for an empowering, public-good civic financial resilience companion. Its mission is to demystify complex financial misinformation, fraudulent solicitations, and emotional impulse investing by delivering an ethos of "Pause. Understand. Reflect. Decide for yourself." It addresses everyday citizens, first-time investors, and regional language speakers seeking calm clarity in high-pressure financial contexts.

The visual style blends **Modern Corporate Clarity** with **Warm Humanist Approachability**:
- **Structured Trust:** Grounded in deep navy bedrock tones, crisp semantic status indicators, and institutional typography that instills high credibility and regulatory safety.
- **Supportive Warmth:** Soft tinted surfaces (indigo, lavender, and slate mists) prevent financial anxiety, shifting visual tone from stressful enterprise banking to reassuring guidance.
- **Vibrant Accent Energy:** Electric royal blues transitioning into radiant cyan gradients signify active AI intelligence, proactive protection, and modern digital literacy.
- **Human Connection:** Integrated companion presence, expressive visual aids, and multi-language support (English, Hindi, Tamil) create a welcoming environment that treats the user as an autonomous decision-maker rather than a consumer target.

## Colors

The palette establishes a balance of institutional safety, dynamic guidance, and calm visual hygiene.

### Primary, Secondary & Accent Palette
- **Primary (`#2563EB`)**: Royal Electric Blue, anchoring key calls-to-action, active selection states, navigational dominance, and interactive steps.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4`)**: Vivid Cyan / Teal, pairing alongside the primary blue to create an energetic gradient representing forward-looking financial awareness and assistive intelligence.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`)**: Mint Green, designating positive safety signals, verified sources, learned milestones, and safe harbor metrics.
- **Warning & Danger Accents**: Amber Warmth (`#F59E0B`) indicates unverified information and pause prompts; Crimson Alert (`#EF4444`) flags urgent fraud, high-risk solicitation, and manipulative pressure tactics.

### Neutral & Foundation Layers
- **Deep Navy Base (`#0F172A`, `#0A192F`)**: Used for high-contrast primary text, deep-mode voice surfaces, and strong header branding.
- **Muted Slate (`#64748B`, `#475569`)**: Supporting secondary text, metadata labels, and inactive navigation cues.
- **Soft Background Washes (`#F8FAFC`, `#EEF2FF`, `#E0E7FF`)**: Ambient page backgrounds and tinted card clusters that prevent eye fatigue and reduce financial stress.
- **Card Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white card layers framed with subtle structural boundaries (`rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`).

## Typography

The type system prioritizes high-speed comprehension, universal legibility across diverse reading capabilities, and friendly confidence.

- **Headlines & Display:** **Plus Jakarta Sans** brings rounded geometry and open letter apertures, balancing authority with warmth. Display sizes project optimism and reassurance across hero cards, dashboard salutations, and decision review stages.
- **Body Text:** **Inter** is deployed for paragraphs, analytical findings, chat conversations, and regulatory footnotes. Its tall x-height and neutral geometry ensure unstrained readability across extended disclosures and non-Latin character sets (such as Tamil and Devanagari regional scripts).
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Built with **Plus Jakarta Sans** in medium and semibold weights for high-legibility navigation chips, pill buttons, verification stamps, and safety badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is constructed on an 8pt modular rhythm designed to accommodate analytical data cards alongside breathing room for reflective decision-making.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (1024px and above):** 12-column adaptive fluid grid with `gutter-desktop` (24px) and page margins of `margin-desktop` (32px). Standard layouts leverage an integrated side-dock navigation rail (240px–280px fixed width) paired with a responsive fluid content well.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `gutter` (20px) and `margin-tablet` (24px). Sidebars collapse to responsive top headers or sliding off-canvas drawers.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters and `margin` (16px). Cards span the full width, buttons convert to sticky bottom bars, and interactive decision trees step vertically.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Tight relationships (icon to text, chip internal padding): `space-xs` (4px) to `space-sm` (8px).
- Component interior padding and adjacent field gaps: `space-md` (16px).
- Card body cushioning, segment boundaries, and stack grouping: `space-lg` (24px).
- Major container separators and hero dashboard headers: `space-xl` (32px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is conveyed through soft, ambient layered depth combined with clean perimeter containment. Heavy cast shadows are avoided in favor of calm, clinical clarity.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Base (Level 0):** Flat background in subtle cool gray or lavender mist (`#F8FAFC` to `#EEF2FF`).
2. **Standard Content Cards (Level 1):** Solid white surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a micro-border of `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.85)` and an ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
3. **Interactive & Active Cards (Level 2):** Elevated cards on hover or focus: `box-shadow: 0 10px 24px -4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`, slightly accentuating primary blue luminance.
4. **Floating Action Controls & Voice Pods (Level 3):** Modal sheets, multi-language audio triggers, and the voice interface overlay use an ambient luminous drop: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`.
5. **Night/Voice Assistant Realm:** Dark-mode audio screens shift into deep cosmic slate (`#0A192F` to `#0F172A`) paired with inner cyan radial glows (`radial-gradient(circle, rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25) 0%, transparent 70%)`) to express active listening.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates generous, organic curvatures to reinforce safety, reassurance, and approachability. 

- **Primary Radius (`roundedness: 2` / 16px):** Applied to form fields, informational cards, and standard containers.
- **Large Container Radius (`20px - 28px`):** Used for focal analytical cards, hero modules, onboarding carousels, and visual message check zones.
- **Full Pill Radius (`9999px`):** Standard for all interactive CTA buttons, status tags, category filter chips, search input frames, and user language switchers. This roundness softens financial interactions, signaling safe exploration.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action Pill:** Features a dynamic horizontal gradient from Electric Blue (`#2563EB`) to Cyan (`#06B6D4`). Text is pure white bold Plus Jakarta Sans. Subtle forward arrow icon placed on the trailing edge. Hover induces a gentle glow lift.
- **Secondary / Neutral Pill:** Solid white surface with an outline border (`1.5px solid #CBD5E1`) and navy typography (`#0F172A`). Hover transitions background to `#F8FAFC`.
- **Text & Ghost Actions:** Deep navy or royal blue text with no background. Active states show a transparent blue wash (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.08)`).

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Built as full pill or 16px rounded enclosures. Backgrounds are clean `#FFFFFF` with borders in `#E2E8F0`. Focus state rings outward with `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)` and a primary blue border.
- Integrated search bars feature a trailing audio microphone or language selector icon.

### Cards & Analytical Containers
- Built with 20px–28px rounded corners, solid white fill, and subtle grey perimeter lines.
- Warning cards incorporate a full-bleed top warning banner (tinted amber `#FEF3C7` or crimson `#FEE2E2`) with bold safety headlines, icon badges, and structured bullet insights.
- Educational summary cards feature dual-column illustrated walkthroughs paired with step-by-step reasoning markers.

### Chips, Badges & Safety Signals
- **Status Indicators:** Pill-shaped tags with tinted pastel fills and high-contrast text:
  - *Safe / Verified:* Mint green fill (`#DCFCE7`) with emerald text (`#15803D`).
  - *Caution / Verification Needed:* Amber fill (`#FEF3C7`) with warm ochre text (`#B45309`).
  - *Alert / Suspicious:* Rose fill (`#FFE4E6`) with crimson text (`#BE123C`).
- **Language & Filter Chips:** Rounded pill chips with inactive slate borders (`#E2E8F0`), transforming to solid `#2563EB` fill with white text when toggled.

### Checkboxes, Radio Controls & Step Selectors
- Radio and multi-select choices are surfaced as full-width interactive option cards. Selected options transition from neutral borders to a 2px royal blue perimeter with a soft blue background wash (`#EFF6FF`).

### Decision Helper ("Pause & Reflect" Tree)
- Multi-step horizontal progress stepper displaying numeric stages connected by continuous tracker lines.
- Reflection questions utilize open card layouts paired with gentle encouragement hints, removing all coercive countdowns or artificial friction.
