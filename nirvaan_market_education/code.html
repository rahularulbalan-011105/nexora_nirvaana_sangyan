What is Volatility?

சந்தை ஏற்ற இறக்கம் • बाजार में उतार-चढ़ाव

Volatility simply measures how much and how quickly an asset price moves up and down over a given timeframe. High volatility indicates sharper swings in brief windows; low volatility indicates steadier, gradual shifts.
